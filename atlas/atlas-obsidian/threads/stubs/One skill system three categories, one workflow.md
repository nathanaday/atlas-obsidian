Work on all of the skills for the project. The goal is a clean skill system with clear categorization and documentation. Each skill needs to be reviewed and assessed to create one cohesive workflow. Some skills were pulled from other open source projects, especially the wiki skills; they are decent, but they must integrate into our ecosystem. Own the entire skill system: clean, neat categories with one design concept, without wasted redundancy between them and without gaps in the workflow. It must feel well thought out, well tested, and easy to understand. Covers the planned Atlas Merge, Atlas Review (quick and deep), Atlas Ingest (huge documents, map-reduce with subagents), Atlas Onboard (existing or blank project, propose the wiki structure, gather ongoing threads), and Atlas Work (move a thread to its next stage, small commits, subagents).
